About

ICC Jerusalem

www.iccjer.co.il

International Convention Center

The Jerusalem International Convention Center, Binyaney Ha’ooma, is the largest center in Israel where you can hold diverse events with a large number of participants.

Due to its unique structure, it is also possible to hold different events at the Center simultaneously without any one of them interfering with another. The Convention Center’s professional staff can help you plan the event, from the initial conceptual stage through to the completion of the event and the departure of the last participant – and all to your complete satisfaction.

The Building
The Center is located at the entrance to Jerusalem and is extremely convenient and accessible.
It contains 27 halls of varying sizes offering great flexibility for hosting 100 -20,000 participants.
The halls have the finest state-of-the art equipment for events such as conferences, congresses, trade fairs, seminars, symposia, social encounters, business events, and private events.
The Ussishkin Auditorium is the largest in the country, with 3,000 seats for concerts and other performances.
There are 12,000 square meters (130,000 square feet) of exhibition space and halls spread over ten different spaces on two levels.
Large parking areas adjacent to the Center, including covered parking.
